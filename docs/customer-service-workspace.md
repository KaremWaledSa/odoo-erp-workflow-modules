# Customer 360 & Service Workspace

## Problem

Customer-service users needed one operational view that collected the most relevant customer context and exposed follow-up work without navigating across many disconnected screens.

## Solution

I developed an Odoo workspace that organized customer information, service activity, and operational indicators into a clearer Customer 360 workflow. The module used Odoo models, relational fields, actions, menus, list views, form views, filters, and business rules to keep the user flow consistent with the rest of the ERP.

## Responsibilities

- Translate operational requirements into Odoo objects and user actions.
- Design the navigation and information hierarchy for customer-service users.
- Implement and connect models, views, fields, filters, and status behavior.
- Validate the flow with realistic business scenarios and refine usability.

## Skills demonstrated

Odoo ERP development, Python business logic, XML views, relational data modeling, requirements analysis, and user-centered workflow design.
